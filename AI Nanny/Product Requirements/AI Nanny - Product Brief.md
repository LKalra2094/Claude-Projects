# Product Brief: AI Nanny

**Created**: April 2026
**Status**: Closed

---

## Opportunity

### Problem Statement

Young children (ages 4-10) are naturally curious and ask dozens of questions daily. Parents can't always answer in the moment — they're cooking, driving, working. The child either gets "ask me later" (which kills curiosity) or turns to a screen (YouTube, voice assistants) that speaks in a robotic or stranger's voice with no safety guardrails designed for children.

The gap: there's no product that answers a child's questions in real-time, in a voice they trust (their parent's), with content filtered for age-appropriateness.

### Supporting Evidence

**Personal Validation:** Parents routinely report being overwhelmed by the volume of questions from young children. "Why is the sky blue?" is charming the first time — less so at question #47 during a road trip.

**Market Signals:** The children's edtech market is valued at $30B+. Voice assistants (Alexa, Siri) are already used by children but lack parental voice, child-specific safety, and age-appropriate answer formatting.

**Structural Root Cause:** Voice assistants are designed for adults. Retrofitting child safety onto adult products produces awkward guardrails. A child-first product with parent-voice trust is a different design paradigm.

---

## Target Audience

- Parents of children ages 4-10 who want to encourage curiosity
- Parents who are uncomfortable with children using generic voice assistants
- Children who are in the "why?" phase and want immediate, trustworthy answers
- Families where screen time is a concern but learning is valued

---

## Goals

### Qualitative

- A child should feel like they're talking to a warm, patient version of their parent
- Parents should feel confident their child won't hear inappropriate content
- The experience should feel magical — speak a question, hear your parent's voice answer

### Quantitative

- End-to-end response time under 6 seconds
- Child-appropriate answers 100% of the time (safety filter never fails)
- Successful voice clone that parent confirms sounds like them

---

## Non-Goals

| What We're NOT Solving | Why |
|------------------------|-----|
| Multi-language support | English-only for hackathon MVP |
| Parent authentication / accounts | Demo mode, single hardcoded voice |
| Persistent conversation storage | Client-side memory is sufficient for demo |
| Mobile native app | Web app via Lovable is faster to build |
| COPPA compliance | Legal requirement for production, not for hackathon |
| Parental dashboard / usage review | V2.0 feature, not needed for demo |
